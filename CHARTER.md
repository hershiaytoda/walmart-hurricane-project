## Part 4: How we work

### Where we talk
We communicate mainly in an iMessage group chat and work together in person during class. We expect a reply within 24-36 hours unless a notice of absence has been given.

### When we meet
Outside of class, we meet **Mondays and Wednesdays, 11:00-11:45 AM, right after lecture**, if needed. [TODO: confirm this is on everyone's calendar.]

### How we decide
1. Discuss it as a whole group. Each side explains its context and reasoning, and we try to reach agreement through discussion.
2. If that fails, we look at the data and go with the justification the data supports best.
3. If the data doesn't settle it, the owner of that part of the project makes the final call, and it goes in the decision log with the reason.

### If someone goes quiet
- After one day with no response, check in with them directly.
- After two days, bring it to the whole team.
- If it's still unresolved, email the professor. We won't wait until the deadline.
